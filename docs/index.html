ImanTrack

Your minimalist, distraction-free companion for daily Adhkar, Tasbih, and Quran reading goals – beautifully aligned with the Sunnah.

Download on the App Store

Key Features

🌙 Sunnah-Aligned Reset

Your tracking day does not reset arbitrarily at midnight. Following traditional Islamic practice, it resets automatically at sunset (Maghrib), calculated using your location.

✨ Daily Adhkar

Perform your Morning, Evening, and Sleep Adhkar with a fluid interface. Use the auto-advance feature to easily transition through each supplication without distractions.

📿 Advanced Tasbih & Custom Dhikr

Choose from authentic preset Tasbihs or easily build your own custom Dhikr. Includes a haptic counter and a customizable Auto-Counter with adjustable BPM tempo controls.

📖 Quran Reader

Read the Quran in the beautiful, authentic "KFGQPC Hafs Script" calligraphy. Features a clean layout, pinch-to-zoom for sizing, and automatic progress saving.

📅 Surah Al-Kahf Reminder

Track your weekly Friday reading of Surah Al-Kahf with a dedicated progress indicator and integrated reader, active from Thursday sunset to Friday sunset.

🔒 100% Private & Ad-Free

No ads, no accounts, and no data tracking. All your records and stats are stored entirely offline on your device. Quiet, private, and lightweight.

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: June 20, 2026

Your privacy is of utmost importance. ImanTrack is designed to be fully private, local, and secure.

1. Data Collection

ImanTrack does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data, usage data, or credentials on external servers. All information generated by the app remains strictly on your device.

2. Location Data

The app requests access to your approximate location to calculate solar times (Maghrib/Fajr) for prayer resets and alarms. This location calculation is performed entirely offline on your device. Your location is never shared, sent, or sold.

3. Third-Party Services

The app does not integrate any third-party SDKs, tracking tools, analytics platforms, or advertisement networks. We do not track your behavior.

4. App Permissions

  • Location: Used offline to calculate solar times.
  • Notifications: Used locally to trigger your Fajr alarm. No push notifications from external servers are sent.

5. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at support@imantrack.com.
